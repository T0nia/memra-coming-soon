Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Memra ("Memra", "we", "our", or "us"), operated by Realia Support Services, collects, uses, stores, protects, and otherwise processes information in connection with the Memra mobile application and related services.

By using Memra, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Memra may collect information including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account information
  • Transaction information
  • Information relating to recipients and transactions
  • Payment confirmations and transaction references
  • Device and technical information
  • IP address
  • App usage and diagnostic information
  • Information required for identity or transaction verification
  • Communications you send to our customer support team

We will only collect information that is reasonably necessary for providing, securing, improving, or legally operating the Memra service.

2. Payment Information

Payments made through Memra may be processed through third-party payment providers, including Paystack and relevant financial institutions or payment partners.

Memra does not intend to store users' full debit-card numbers, card security codes, PINs, or online-banking passwords on its own systems.

Payment providers may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Memra to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Facilitate escrow transactions
  • Process and confirm payments
  • Generate transaction and release codes
  • Verify transactions and users where necessary
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Investigate suspicious transactions
  • Resolve disputes
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Monitor service performance and security
  • Communicate important service information
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

4. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with third parties where reasonably necessary to provide or secure the service, including:

  • Payment processors
  • Financial institutions and payment partners
  • Technology and infrastructure providers
  • Security, fraud-prevention, and verification providers
  • Professional advisers where necessary
  • Government, regulatory, or law-enforcement authorities where legally required

We do not sell users' personal information as a business practice.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, we expect them to apply appropriate safeguards and process information only for legitimate purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal and transaction information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the service, maintaining transaction records, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, complying with legal obligations, and meeting applicable regulatory requirements.

Some information may therefore remain after an account is deleted where retention is required or permitted by law.

Users may request deletion of their Memra account and associated personal information through the account-deletion process provided by Memra.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution, accounting, or transaction-record purposes.

Requests may be submitted through the contact information provided below.
